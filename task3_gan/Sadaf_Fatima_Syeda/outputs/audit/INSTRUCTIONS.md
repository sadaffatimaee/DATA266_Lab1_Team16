# Human audit

Each image in images/ shows the input photo (left) and the Monet-style translation (right).
Score each sample 1-5 on your own, without looking at the other sheet:

- style: how much it looks like a Monet painting (5 = very Monet)
- content: how well the scene of the photo is kept (5 = fully kept)
- artifacts: visual defects (5 = no artifacts, 1 = severe)
